# Report on “FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week”

## Purpose of the Event
* Showcase cutting-edge Agentic AI solutions developed during the Agentic AI Build Week competition.
* Highlight the strategic value of continuous learning and innovation in shaping the future of tech in Vietnam and Asia, led by AWS leadership.
* Explore real-world multi-agent architectures applied to conversational commerce, business competitive intelligence, automated cloud architecture, and financial compliance.
* Bridge theoretical AI knowledge with practical business applications that drive scalability and automation.

## Speakers
* **Giuseppe Marazzotta** – Head of Tech & Solution Architecture, ASEAN @ Amazon Web Services (AWS).
* **One Team** – Winners of the AWS Track (*AI-Powered Conversational Ordering*).
* **Final Scout** – Project Team (*Multi-Agent Competitive Intelligence*).
* **3K** – Project Team (*AI Architect Assistant*).
* **Six Pillar** – Project Team (*Adaptive Workflow Engine*).

## Key Highlights

### Main Content

#### Keynote: The Power of Continuous Learning & Regional Innovation (Giuseppe Marazzotta)
* Emphasized that attendees and developers in the room are the ones who will innovate, transform traditional industries, and build the future of Vietnam and Asia.
* Encouraged participants to continuously invest in themselves and expand their knowledge far beyond single events or short-term training sessions.
* Highlighted that individuals who commit to being lifelong learners will inevitably achieve long-term success in both their personal lives and technical careers.
* Expressed gratitude and privilege to join the community on a Saturday morning, reiterating AWS’s commitment to empowering local talent.

#### AWS Track Winner: AI-Powered Conversational Ordering (One Team)
* Developed an AI agent system designed to streamline the ordering journey for fast-food businesses (such as KFC).
* Built on a flexible, modular architecture that enables seamless adaptation to diverse customer communication channels (e.g., Zalo, WhatsApp) without requiring core system rebuilds.

#### Multi-Agent Competitive Intelligence (Final Scout)
* Created an autonomous multi-agent platform that scrapes, aggregates, and synthesizes scattered public data about business competitors (such as financial statements and investor transcripts).
* Utilized AI agents to analyze market data, evaluate competitive strategies, and deliver actionable insights for executive management, risk assessment, and long-term business planning.

#### AI Architect Assistant (3K)
* Addressed the operational friction in designing cloud infrastructure and calculating accurate cost estimations.
* Allowed users to input unstructured requirements via free-text prompts or business documents, automatically generating cloud architecture diagrams and deployment-ready Terraform code aligned with corporate best practices and templates.

#### Adaptive Workflow Engine for Financial Compliance (Six Pillar)
* Engineered an Anti-Money Laundering (AML) automation solution tailored for financial institutions.
* Deployed specialized AI agents to handle Know Your Customer (KYC) screening, transaction anomaly analysis, and compliance evidence building.
* Drastically reduced manual review time for analysts, dramatically improving throughput, review speed, and scalability in regulatory compliance.

## Key Learnings

### Design & Operational Mindset
* Adopt a lifelong growth mindset technology stacks and AI models evolve rapidly, making continuous learning the single greatest asset for engineers.
* Design for adaptability; modular architecture ensures AI solutions can easily integrate with new messaging channels or enterprise tools without costly refactoring.

### Technical & AI Architecture
* Multi-agent orchestration is superior for complex, domain-specific tasks (e.g., financial compliance or market research) compared to single monolith prompts.
* Combining Generative AI with Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC) generation dramatically reduces human error and enforces organizational architectural standards.

### Business Impact & Automation
* AI agents deliver the highest ROI when targeting high-friction manual processes like document parsing, competitive analysis, and transaction auditing.
* Operational speed and compliance scalability can be achieved simultaneously when repetitive human checks are replaced by specialized agent workflows.

---

## Application to Work

* **In software & AI engineering:**
  * Build agentic workflows with modular interfaces so that core reasoning engines remain decoupled from external channels (e.g., Zalo, Webhooks, APIs).
  * Break down large business problems into specialized multi-agent roles (e.g., Data Collector, Strategy Analyst, Compliance Auditor) for higher output quality.
* **In cloud infrastructure:**
  * Utilize AI-driven IaC generators to accelerate cloud provisioning while embedding governance, security rules, and cost optimization templates by default.
* **In career growth & leadership:**
  * Embrace a culture of lifelong learning, actively applying new AI capabilities to daily engineering workflows and sharing knowledge back with the tech community.
