## Areas for Improvement

* **Enhance Operational Focus & Discipline:** Maintain unwavering focus on single tasks to avoid context-switching and distractions during complex debugging sessions.
* **Sharpen Problem-Solving Depth:** Shift from temporary fixes to root-cause analysis ("No-Blame Post-Mortem" approach) when addressing operational and system bugs.
* **Proactive & Clear Communication:** Express technical roadblocks early and articulate issues more clearly to mentors and team members.
* **Practical Tech Application:** Focus on building hands-on projects and tangible implementations when learning new tools, moving beyond purely theoretical understanding.
* **Peer Support & Mentorship:** Actively assist teammates with technical challenges and foster mutual learning within the team.
